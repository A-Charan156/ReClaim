The Frustration of Lost Items

College campuses often grapple with inefficient lost-and-found processes, relying on outdated, manual methods that lead to frustration and wasted time.

Wasted Time

Students waste valuable time and energy searching for misplaced belongings. The average American reportedly loses 2.5 days per year looking for lost items.

Lack of Centralization

Current systems lack a centralized database, easy reporting mechanisms, and secure communication channels, making item recovery difficult.

Fragmented Systems

Relying on scattered emails or physical offices creates fragmentation and reduces the chances of successfully recovering lost items.

How LostNFound Works

Our intuitive workflow guides users from registration to successful item recovery, making the process seamless and efficient.

Two-Way Posting

Users can easily post both lost and found items, ensuring comprehensive coverage across campus.

Automated Matching

An intelligent algorithm compares item details: category, description, location, and time for accurate pairing.

Privacy-First Design

Double-blind communication protects user identities until consent for contact is given.

Real-Time Notifications

Instant alerts inform users of potential matches, speeding up the recovery process.

Admin Dashboard

Campus staff gain a powerful tool to monitor and manage all reports efficiently.

Intuitive UI/UX

Designed with a focus on simplicity, ease of use, and accessibility for all campus users.
